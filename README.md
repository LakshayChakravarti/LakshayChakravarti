## 📄 Research & Publications

### A Sentence-Level Risk Estimator for Identifying Hallucinations in Generative AI

**Publisher:** IEEE

Contributed to the research and writing of a framework for estimating hallucination risk at the sentence level in generative AI outputs.

- Explores sentence-level detection of factually incorrect or unsupported generated content.
- Combines BERT-based semantic similarity, question-answering-based factuality verification, and Natural Language Inference (NLI).
- Investigates a unified Sentence Risk Score for assessing the reliability of generated statements.

**Research Areas:** Generative AI · NLP · LLM Reliability · Factuality Assessment

📖 [Read the paper on IEEE Xplore](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11441054)
